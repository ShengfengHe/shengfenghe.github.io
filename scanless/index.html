01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Not every missing surface needs to be scanned.

Conventional scanning maximizes coverage. ScanLess asks a harder question: which new measurement will most improve the final human reconstruction? The answer depends on clothing, shape ambiguity, current coverage and acquisition cost.

01

Observe

Preserve reliable geometry from Eva. Track measured surfaces, scanner viewpoints, registration confidence and time.

Measurement is evidence.
02

Infer

Use a human-specific shape and clothing prior to predict missing geometry—along with calibrated uncertainty, not false certainty.

A plausible surface is not ground truth.
03

Guide & stop

Recommend a short, reachable scanner movement that improves quality per second. Stop when the desired quality threshold is met.

Time is part of the objective.
Traditional workflow
Scan → scan more → finish

The operator tries to observe the entire person, often revisiting already adequate surfaces and spending time on predictable regions.

Proposed learning-guided workflow
Scan → predict → decide → stop

The assistant distinguishes regions worth measuring from regions that can be completed reliably, while preserving real observations.

04 / TRAINING DATA

Complete scans are the simulator's teacher.

A complete mesh makes every possible simulated observation measurable. We can calculate how much a candidate next view would actually improve reconstruction—without rescan sessions for each decision.

Recommended data recipe

3DHumans ↗~180 clothed human meshes captured with Artec Eva. Best primary source for realistic Eva-shaped surfaces; access requires a signed research agreement. [6]
THuman2.0 ↗500 high-quality human scans from a dense DSLR rig. Useful for diversity, but not Eva measurements; approval and non-commercial restrictions apply. [7]
Real Eva framesRecord 10–20 complete sessions with raw/registered frame sequences, estimated transforms and failures. Use these to calibrate real sensor noise and tracking behavior.
Held-out subjectsReserve 20–30 unseen people for real-world evaluation. Train/test separation must be by human identity, not partial-scan trajectory.
INITIAL SCALE · PLANNING TARGET

Thousands of virtual decisions. A handful of real sessions.

150–300

05 / METHOD NOVELTY

The model learns when not to scan.

Point-cloud completion and next-best-view planning already exist. The method must make scanning decisions that meaningfully exploit a human-specific completion prior, actual measurement reliability and operator movement constraints.

CONTRIBUTION 01

Measured-vs-inferred geometry

Maintain an evidence mask. Preserve Eva's measured details while generating only missing surfaces. Estimate error or uncertainty on unseen regions, especially loose clothing and small parts.

CONTRIBUTION 02 · PRIMARY

Completion-aware next view

Score reachable scan actions by expected reduction in final surface error per unit acquisition time—rather than raw unseen-surface coverage.

CONTRIBUTION 03

Quality-aware stopping

Stop when predicted remaining error falls below an application-defined target. Validate the policy's confidence instead of equating a complete-looking mesh with an accurate one.

next_action = argmax [ expected quality gain / action time − λ × tracking risk ]Conceptual objective. The training oracle may use full-mesh ground truth; the deployed policy cannot.

KEY GO / NO-GO
Can Eva provide usable registered observations while scanning?

If yes, build the live assistant with the SDK. If no, continue simulation/offline research with exported Artec Studio frames, but explicitly defer the real-time claim and request vendor integration support. The timings above are illustrative project stages, not delivery promises.

STUDENT HANDOFF

The first ten working days.

The most useful initial output is not a trained model. It is a small technical feasibility package proving that we can read Eva frames, replay a short session and compare a few simulated scan paths.

Research priority: data access and validation. Establish reliable frame streaming and an offline next-best-view benchmark before scaling to a complete operator-facing system.
